Is it free?

Yes. Prayer times, reminders, the Qibla, the calendar, the live sky with today’s weather and the Home Screen widgets are free, and always will be. Horizon Pro adds the Lock Screen widgets, the Live Activity, the forecast for the days ahead and a heads-up before each prayer.

Why is there a Pro at all?

So Horizon can stay independent and ad-free. Pro pays for the weather data, Apple’s developer fees, and the time to keep making it better. Prayer itself never costs anything.

How much is Pro?

$3.99 a month or $19.99 a year, each with a week free, or $59.99 once. Family Sharing is included. Prices vary by country.

Which iPhones does it run on?

Any iPhone with iOS 18 or later. On iOS 26 it uses Liquid Glass.

Which calculation methods does it support?

Muslim World League, ISNA, the Moonsighting Committee, Umm al-Qura, Egyptian, Karachi, Dubai, Qatar, Kuwait, Kemenag, JAKIM, MUIS, Diyanet, Tehran and more, with Standard or Hanafi Asr and per-prayer offsets.

Why does it ask for my location?

To work out your prayer times and the Qibla on your iPhone. It also goes to Apple to name your city and fetch the weather. You can choose a city instead and never be asked.

What does it know about me?

Only your settings, kept on your iPhone. There’s no account. Nothing reaches me unless you send feedback, and then only your note, your city and your method, so I can check your times. Apple receives your location to name your city and get the weather. The privacy policy has every detail.
