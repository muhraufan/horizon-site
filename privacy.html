Horizon Get the app

Privacy. Horizon has no account, no ads and no analytics. It doesn't track you, and it doesn't sell or share anything about you. This page says exactly what it does with your information, in plain words.

Updated 13 November 2026. Horizon is made by Muhammad Raufan Yusup, an independent developer in Japan.

The short version

  • Your prayer times are calculated on your iPhone.
  • Your settings stay on your iPhone.
  • Two things go to Apple, and only to Apple: your location, to name your city and to get the weather. Neither is linked to you by Horizon.
  • Nothing comes to me unless you send me feedback, and then only what the feedback page shows you.
  • If you give someone a year of Pro, Horizon's gift server keeps the purchase's transaction ID beside the code it gave you, and nothing about either of you.

Location

With Automatic location on, Horizon uses your iPhone's location to work out prayer times and the Qibla. That happens on your iPhone. To show your city's name, Horizon asks Apple's location service, which receives your coordinates. Apple handles them under Apple's Privacy Policy.

You can choose a city yourself instead. Then Horizon never asks for your location at all, and nothing about where you are leaves your iPhone except for the weather, below.

Weather

Horizon gets the forecast from Apple Weather, so Apple receives the location of your chosen place when the weather is fetched. Horizon keeps the forecast on your iPhone for up to three days, so the sky works offline.

What's stored on your iPhone

Your settings (place, calculation method, offsets, reminders and units), the saved forecast, and a note of whether you have Horizon Pro. They're shared only with Horizon's own widgets, on the same iPhone. Deleting Horizon deletes them. If you back up your iPhone, they're part of that backup, as with any app.

Reminders, widgets and the Live Activity

Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. Widgets and the Live Activity are drawn on your iPhone from the same settings. None of them use a server.

Purchases

Horizon Pro is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment, and Horizon never sees your payment details. Horizon checks your purchase with Apple on your iPhone. Family Sharing works the same way.

If you give a year of Pro

When you buy a gift, Horizon sends Apple's signed record of the purchase to Horizon's gift server at horizon.muhraufan.com, which runs on Cloudflare. The server checks Apple's signature and hands back one gift code from a batch made in App Store Connect, with a short link to it.

The server keeps only the purchase's transaction ID, the code and link it gave out, and when. That stops one purchase being used for two codes. It doesn't receive your name, the recipient's name, your message or your Apple Account. Those stay on your iPhone, on the card you send. Cloudflare handles the requests under Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

Whoever redeems the code does it with Apple, in the App Store or in Horizon, so the server never learns who received it.

If you send feedback

Horizon's feedback page sends your note only when you tap Send. It includes:

  • The kind of note you chose, and what you wrote.
  • For a time that's off, the prayers and minutes you set.
  • A screenshot, only if you add one.
  • Your email address, only if you type it in for a reply.
  • Horizon's version, your iOS version, your iPhone's model and the language Horizon is in.
  • Your place, calculation method, Asr rule and offsets, unless you switch that off on the page.

The note goes to a Google Sheet in my own Google account, through Google Apps Script, and Google handles it under Google's Privacy Policy. A screenshot is kept in my Google Drive. Only I read them, and I use them only to understand the note and reply. Nothing is linked to an account, because there isn't one. To have a note deleted, write to me.

If you email me, I receive what you send and your email address, and use them only to read and reply. I don't add you to any list.

If you test Horizon through TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and any feedback you choose to send with me, under TestFlight's terms.

If you ask for an email at launch

The form on this website sends the email address you type, and your browser's language, only when you tap Email me at launch. It goes to the same Google Sheet as feedback, on its own tab. I use it only to send you one email when Horizon is on the App Store, then delete the list. It's never shared or added to any other list. To be taken off before then, write to me.

If you ask for an email when Horizon is on Android

The Android form on this website sends the email address you type, and your browser’s language, only when you tap Email me at launch. It goes to Horizon’s server at horizon.muhraufan.com, which runs on Cloudflare, and is kept with the day you added it. I use it only to send you one email when Horizon is on Google Play, in your language where I can, then delete the list. It’s never shared or added to any other list. Cloudflare handles the request under Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy. To be taken off before then, write to me.

This website

horizon.muhraufan.com counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, so I can see how many people come and which pages they read. It uses no cookies, and it can’t tell who you are or follow you to other sites. Cloudflare handles it under Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy. This is only the website: the app itself has no analytics.

Children

Horizon collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will change first, with a new date at the top. Horizon will never start collecting anything without saying so here.
